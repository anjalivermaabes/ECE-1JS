A laptop is a portable, all-in-one personal computer with a built-in screen, keyboard, and touchpad. It runs on a rechargeable battery and AC power, allowing for use in various locations, and integrates all essential components into a single, compact unit. These are often called notebooks and can be used for tasks like browsing the internet, creating documents, and playing games, similar to a desktop but with the added benefit of mobility
